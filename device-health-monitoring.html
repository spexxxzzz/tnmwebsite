Device Health Monitoring

We create health-monitoring layers that watch internal machine signals continuously, detect degradation early, and surface operating risk before a system drifts into failure.

What This CoversContinuous state monitoring, anomaly detection, health scoring, degradation tracking, and alerting tied to machine behavior.

Technical Scope

  • Monitoring pipelines for internal signals such as vibration, temperature, pressure, current, torque, or device-specific telemetry.
  • Trend analysis and change-point detection that identify slow drift as well as abrupt abnormal behavior.
  • Threshold and rule layers combined with learned anomaly scoring for more robust device-state evaluation.
  • Health-index generation to summarize current condition and expose movement toward degraded operation.
  • Operator-facing and system-facing alerts that can trigger review, maintenance, or runtime safeguards.

Typical Deliverables

Monitoring dashboards, anomaly thresholds, device health scores, and integration with alarms or supervisory control paths.

Why It Matters

Health visibility reduces unplanned downtime and makes runtime decision systems aware of the machine’s true condition.

Integration Pattern

Telemetry stream → health analytics → state classification → alerting, operator visibility, or automated safety response.
